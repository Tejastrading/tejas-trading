Who We Are

One partner for many kinds of raw materials.

Tejas Trading is a Kolkata, West Bengal based trading and export company dealing in a broad and evolving range of raw materials.

We are deliberately not a single-industry supplier. Our strength is the breadth of the product portfolio and the ability to support different industries through one sourcing partner: from Ayurvedic and natural ingredients to food chemicals, bakery inputs, cosmetic and personal-care chemicals, essential oils, fragrances, rose water, saffron and natural flavours, and food colours.

The business carries more than 25 years of experience in the trade. Formal incorporation under the name Tejas Trading followed approximately two years ago, giving long-standing trade relationships an organised business foundation. Alongside our sourced range, we are manufacturers in our own right: rose water, kesar and kewra preparations, cardamom flavours, essential oils and perfumery compounds are produced by Tejas Trading.

Kolkata is where we are based, and where a first call or email begins. What we supply is judged by one standard: whether it helps your business make its products reliably.
