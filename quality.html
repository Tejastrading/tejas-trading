Our Standards

Sourcing you can rely on.

Raw-material buyers shouldn't have to take a supplier's word for it. Tejas Trading keeps its approach simple: trade under the licences and norms that genuinely apply, and state nothing the company can't stand behind.

If your production needs particular quality parameters from a material, raise it at the start. We confirm what genuinely applies and stay clear about where something is not available.

Everything stated on this page reflects the company's own confirmed details. No more, no less.

What We Don't Claim

Honesty is part of the specification.

Manufacturer, unless true

Where we manufacture, we say so: rose water, kesar, kewra and cardamom preparations, essential oils and perfumery compounds. Everything else is presented as sourced material.

Markets, as they are

India and Nepal are our established markets. Enquiries from beyond are taken on case by case, after a straight feasibility check.

Specs only when confirmed

Product specifications, grades and availability are confirmed against the actual material being supplied, not copied from catalogues.
