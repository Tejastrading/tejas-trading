Two Ways To Buy

One trading desk, both markets.

Domestic supply (India). Manufacturers across India order materials by requirement: single categories or combined consignments, packed and dispatched to your unit.

Export supply (Nepal). Businesses in Nepal order the same raw materials through our export channel. Share the list and quantities and we handle the order end to end.

We currently serve India and Nepal. If you're outside these two countries, phone or write to us: we'll evaluate the requirement honestly and be clear about what's possible today.

How An Export Order Works

From requirement to consignment, in four steps.

Share your requirement

Material, grade or use-case, quantity and destination, over a call or an email.

Availability & quotation

We confirm sourcing availability and share a clear quotation covering only what can actually be supplied.

Confirmation & scheduling

Once the order is confirmed, we lock down quantity, packing format and the dispatch timeline.

Packing & dispatch

Materials packed for transit and dispatched to order, with details shared as the consignment moves.
